A personal site for someone in training is usually either a CV in HTML or a moodboard. Neither is that useful.

The useful version is a **filter**. Research belongs here if it has a question. Marketing belongs here if it has a system and a result. Poems belong here because they are not results.

Comments are welcome. Push back if a claim is doing too much work.
