A ClickUp case study should show hierarchy: Spaces that match how the business actually works, statuses that mean something, and automations that remove nagging.

Write the before (Slack archaeology, missed handoffs) and the after (a single source of truth, dashboards leadership can read). Screenshots help. So does admitting what you refused to automate.
